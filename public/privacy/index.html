Privacy, in plain language.

Your care record belongs to your household. Caregiver Last Action stores names, feed, sleep, medicine and diaper entries in IndexedDB on your device. We do not receive or analyze that record.

Pairing

If you unlock live pairing, the QR invitation establishes a direct WebRTC connection with a one-time secret. Records sent through that connection are encrypted again with AES-GCM. The service has no account database or relay server. Pair only with someone you trust and do not share the pairing code publicly.

Purchase data

Household passes are sold by Sociobot/Dodo, the merchant of record. The app stores your license token and its most recent verification result in local browser storage. Verification sends that token to api.sociobot.in. Payment details never pass through this app.

Network and analytics

There are no advertising trackers, third-party fonts, analytics SDKs or behavioral profiles. Standard deployment server logs may include an IP address and request metadata for reliability and abuse prevention.

Your controls

You can export a JSON backup or CSV at any time. You can delete individual entries from History; the deletion remains in local correction history so a paired device cannot silently restore it. Clearing this site’s browser data removes the local record and license from that device.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to privacy@sociobot.in.
